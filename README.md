I'm Nathan, an MSc student in Computer & Embedded Systems Engineering at TU Delft. I like working close to the hardware: firmware for microcontrollers, FPGA designs, and finding out what a chip gives away through its power consumption. I also enjoy writing performance-focused code, where every cycle counts.

For my bachelor thesis at Leiden University (LIACS) I did a side-channel analysis of the PROACT chip prototype. I captured 160,000 power traces from four cryptographic co-processors running on an FPGA: two AES cores, Ascon and Xoodyak. I wrote the capture firmware in C and the analysis pipelines in Python, covering TVLA, SNR and CPA. All four co-processors showed data-dependent leakage, and a first-order CPA recovered the full AES-128 key from both AES cores.

I'm looking for internships in the Netherlands in embedded systems, firmware, hardware security or software.
